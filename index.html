Developer Recovery

Developer Recovery is a personal backup and restoration utility. It runs on the owner's computer, encrypts selected development files and local application records, and stores recoverable snapshots on an external drive and in the owner's Google Drive account.

The utility uses restic for encrypted snapshots and rclone for Google Drive transfers. It is a personal tool, with no registration service, hosted application server, or customer accounts.

Google authorization allows the utility to create, read, and verify the owner's encrypted backup repository. The backup password remains outside Google Drive. Restored files are staged for review before applications or scheduled jobs are activated.
